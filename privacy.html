Beat Our AI Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 October 2026

Publisher: Callisto Solutions
Contact: rahmanik1228@gmail.com

This policy covers the Beat Our AI Android app, version 1.0.0, package com.beatoireai.tictactoe, as distributed without advertising, analytics, accounts, or in-app purchases.

Data stored on your device

The app keeps the current match in memory while it is open. It does not create an account and it does not send scores, moves, or device identifiers to a developer server. App-controlled cloud backup is disabled.

Data collection

The app does not collect personal information for the developer. It does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, or photo access. It includes no advertising or analytics SDK.

Permissions and connectivity

Optional touch feedback uses the device vibrator. The Android package may list an internet permission because of the app framework. Fonts and gameplay are bundled in the app. This version makes no developer-server requests and can be played offline.

Retention and deletion

Leaving a match or uninstalling the app clears the session. There is no server account to delete.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults. The publisher selects and declares the intended audience in Google Play.

Changes and contact

If a later version adds accounts, ads, analytics, or online features, this policy and the store forms must be updated first. For privacy questions, use rahmanik1228@gmail.com.
