# Beat Our AI — Google Play listing (v1.1.0)

## App name
Beat Our AI

## Short description
Tic Tac Toe with a sharp AI that sometimes slips. Win if you can.

## Full description
Think you can beat the machine? Most rounds it plays hard. Sometimes it blinks. When you actually line up your marks, the win is yours.

Beat Our AI is a portrait Tic Tac Toe game. You are X. The AI is O.

HOW A ROUND FEELS
The AI talks while you play. When you are one move from a line, it hesitates. Close blocks land late. A full board might stay a tie, or the machine might quietly tidy the last square. If you spot that, tap Catch the cheater — it goes in the incident log.

BOARDS
• Classic 3×3
• 4×4, four in a row
• 5×5, five in a row
• Ultimate: nine small boards inside one big board

Every fifth game, and some others, the AI plays nervous and is easier to trap. Track YOU vs AI across rematches. There is no account, no ads, and no in-app purchase. Play stays on your phone.
